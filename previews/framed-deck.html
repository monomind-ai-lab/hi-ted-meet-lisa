THE PROBLEM

Agents write good decks and terrible files.

A deck that needs a build step, a server, or a folder of assets beside it is not a deliverable. It is homework for whoever receives it.

hi ted, meet lisa The 101 2 / 7
THE IDEA

A template is machinery, not material

  • Design tokens, layout components and controller scripts, kept verbatim
  • Every piece of subject matter replaced by a bracketed slot
  • A pattern reference beside it, with the markup that is easy to get wrong
  • A live preview you can open before you commit to a shape

Three steps, one file
