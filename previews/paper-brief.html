報告定位WHAT THIS BRIEF IS

問題不在翻譯品質，在交接The problem is not translation quality. It is handover.

社群目前的痛點多半被描述成「翻譯落後」。但把資料攤開之後會發現，落後的不是翻譯本身，而是原文改版之後沒有人知道哪幾頁失效了。The community usually describes the pain as “translation is behind”. Laid out, the data says something else: what is behind is not the translating but the fact that nobody learns which pages went stale when the source changed.

建議做法Recommendation
對外只公布同步率，覆蓋率降為內部指標。同時在建置流程加入一個檢查：原文檔案更新時，把對應的譯文標記為待更新，並自動開一則 issue。Publish only the sync rate; demote coverage to an internal metric. Add one check to the build: when a source file changes, mark its translations as pending and open an issue automatically.
04

失效的速度HOW FAST IT GOES STALE

一次改版，平均讓多少頁翻譯過期How many translated pages one release expires

3.4×

每完成一頁新翻譯的同時，平均有 3.4 頁既有翻譯因為原文改版而失去同步。示範數字，用來說明趨勢而非精確值。For every page newly translated, 3.4 already-translated pages fall out of sync because the source moved. An illustrative figure, meant to show direction rather than precision.

這個比例說明的是方向：只靠增加譯者，覆蓋率會上升，同步率不會。要改的是通知機制，不是人力配置。The ratio points one way: adding translators raises coverage and leaves sync where it was. What needs fixing is the notification, not the headcount.

時間敏感Time-sensitive
下一個大版發行前完成標記機制，否則這一批新翻譯會在發行當天同時過期，等於白做。Ship the staleness marker before the next major release, or this batch of new translations expires on release day and the work is wasted.

07

待社群決定FOR THE COMMUNITY TO DECIDE

五個需要先有共識的問題Five questions that need agreement first

  • 正式發行的語系門檻是什麼？這一題決定其他所有題目的範圍，建議最先討論。What is the bar for an officially released locale? This one sets the scope of every other question — take it first.
  • 未達門檻的語系要怎麼呈現？下架、標示為社群維護，還是照舊發行但加註同步狀態。How should a locale below the bar be shown? Withdrawn, labelled community-maintained, or published as now with its sync status attached.
  • 原文改版時，譯文要不要一起延後發行？對齊會拖慢原文，不對齊會讓讀者看到不一致的行為。Should a release wait for its translations? Waiting slows the source; not waiting shows readers inconsistent behaviour.
  • 哪些頁面明確不翻譯？把它們排除在分母之外，數字才有意義。Which pages are explicitly not translated? Until they leave the denominator, the numbers mean little.
  • 誰負責維護這份規範？目前沒有任何角色對翻譯制度本身負責。Who owns this policy? No role is currently accountable for the translation process itself.
